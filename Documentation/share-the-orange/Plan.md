# Plan - Share the Orange

## Goal

Ship a free offline sharing activity for kids, families, and seniors. Six orange slices stay on the phone. Each slice goes to the kid or the grandparent. The huge line says who has more.

## Approach

1. App Store Connect has no rejected version waiting.
2. Build Share the Orange in Expo: one screen, six slices, and a saved split.
3. Run the full simulator regression on the Release binary for build 1.
4. Take three native screenshots, then upload that binary and submit it.

## Why this app

Turn timers, picture lists, outing plans, dice, dinner answers, household places, tapped sentences, copy-the-line, room hunts, shape pairs, two-person poses, a week of walks, and dressing choices are already in the store or in this pipeline. Opposite-word apps are crowded. This one does not use a clock, a camera, or a network. The family shares a real orange while the phone keeps the count.
