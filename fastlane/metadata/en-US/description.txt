Share the Orange gives six slices to a kid and a grandparent. Each tap gives the next slice to one of them. The huge line says who has more, or that the orange is even.

Six slices stay on this phone. Take back returns the last slice. Start a new orange when you want to share again.

Share the Orange does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. The slices in the app are original.
